# Colour palettes

Curated palettes. Used in the design step to propose three directions. Rule for all of them: **60% main (surfaces), 30% second, 10% accent**. The accent goes only on primary actions and the one number or state that matters most.

Hex codes marked *est.* could not be read from the source video and were matched by eye; adjust them freely. Contrast was checked with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (body text needs 4.5:1, large text and icons 3:1).

## How to add a palette

When the user says "save this palette" (hex codes, a site, a screenshot or a link), add an entry in the format below: name, roles, mood, best for, source, readable pairings (run `contrast.mjs`). If only a picture or site is given, read the colours from it and mark guesses *est.*

## 1. Calm Fizzy
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Main 60% | Butter Cream | `#F5ECD7` *est.* |
| Second 30% | Forest Green | `#1E3B2F` |
| Accent 10% | Hot Pink | `#FF3EA5` |
- **Mood:** calm and friendly with one playful spark. **Best for:** consumer brands, food and drink, wellness, D2C landing pages.
- **Readable pairings:** Forest Green text on Butter Cream 10.4:1. Button: Forest Green text on Hot Pink is only 3.8:1, white on Hot Pink 3.2:1, so use Hot Pink for large display words, badges and icons, and make the button Forest Green with cream text, or darken the pink.
- Source: @quanovaai reel, Nov 2026.

## 3. Dark SaaS

|---|---|---|

| Second 30% | Electric Blue | `#2F6BFF` |
| Accent 10% | Sunset Orange | `#FF7A1A` |
- **Mood:** modern, focused, technical. **Best for:** AI products, SaaS dashboards, developer tools.
